Shoprite to buy 40% of tech group DNI as Investec takes a stake in its financial arm

The R5.5 billion deals push the retailer further into banking, payments and mobile services. They are expected to close in mid-2027.

By Rolivhuwa · 24 November 2026 · 1 min read

Shoprite announced on Monday that its financial services arm will buy 40% of technology and fintech investor DNI for about R3.6 billion. Investec will invest about R1.9 billion for a 19.99% stake in Shoprite Financial Services Holdings. Together the deals are worth about R5.5 billion.

Shoprite can raise its DNI stake to 100% from the fourth year, and Investec can increase its holding in the financial services company to as much as 40%. Shoprite will keep majority ownership of its financial services business, and Investec will have board representation. The transactions are expected to close in mid-2027.

What it means for shoppers

DNI has interests in fibre, digital content and fintech, and the deal includes a 25% stake in handset distributor 3G Mobile. Shoprite wants to offer more than groceries, including banking, credit, insurance and mobile connectivity, as it prepares to launch banking services.

Shoprite says the Investec arrangement is not exclusive and will not affect its relationships with other banks and financial services providers. It expects the DNI deal to add to its earnings from the 2027 financial year, and Investec expects its investment to add to its earnings from 2028. The deals follow an alliance banking partnership announced about two weeks earlier.

Sources: TimesLIVE, Business Day, ITWeb and TechCentral reports, and Shoprite and Investec announcements.
