Nvidia brings back Jensen Huang as CEO and Wall Street surges

Nvidia shares jumped about 31% on Monday and the Nasdaq had its best day since April 2025. Lawmakers from both parties criticised the decision.

By Rolivhuwa · 24 November 2026 · 1 min read

Nvidia’s board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive on Monday, 11 days after he resigned, and Nvidia shares jumped about 31%. Huang resigned on 12 November after revelations of extramarital affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy, Reuters reported. Nvidia’s board ruled out a national security risk. It reached its decision after meetings with senior US government officials, and President Donald Trump said he had secured a deal with the board to bring Huang back.

Wall Street rebounds

US stocks surged on the news. The S&P 500 rose 4.73% to 7,237.17, the Nasdaq Composite rose 6.51% to 24,089.56 and the Dow Jones rose 3.01% to 49,595.31, according to Reuters. It was the biggest one-day gain for the Nasdaq and the S&P 500 since the market surged after Trump paused his tariff package in April 2025, and the Nasdaq-100 added more than $2 trillion in market value.

The rally followed a seven-day losing streak that took the S&P 500 more than 10% below its record high. Before Monday, the Nasdaq had come close to a bear market as investors worried about the heavy borrowing behind spending on artificial intelligence.

Criticism in Washington

Lawmakers from both parties criticised Nvidia’s decision. They said Congress should tighten restrictions on China’s access to the company’s advanced AI chips.

Sources: Reuters, AP and Euronews reports.
