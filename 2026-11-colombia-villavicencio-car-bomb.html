Car bomb injures at least 19 near government offices in Colombia’s Villavicencio

No one was killed in the Monday night blast outside the Meta governor’s office, and no group has claimed it.

By Rolivhuwa · 24 November 2026 · 1 min read

A car bomb exploded outside the headquarters of the Meta department government in Villavicencio, central-eastern Colombia, at about 9:30pm on Monday 23 November. Police said at least 19 people were wounded, and some reports said dozens. No deaths had been reported by Tuesday morning.

Five of the injured were security guards working at the government headquarters, and nearby buildings were damaged. President Abelardo de la Espriella confirmed the attack. Police have not said who planted the bomb, and no group had claimed responsibility.

Part of a wider pattern

Villavicencio is the capital of Meta department. It is the latest car bomb attack in Colombia in recent months. Similar attacks hit the south-west of the country in August and the north-east in September.

Sources: Xinhua, The Express Tribune, AFP via News.az and Rio Times reports.
