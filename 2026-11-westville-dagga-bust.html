Man arrested in Westville after R1.5 million dagga bust

Police say a 32-year-old suspect abandoned a vehicle carrying about 320kg of cannabis and ran into nearby bushes after officers tracked it along the N2 and M19.

By Rolivhuwa · 15 November 2026 · 2 min read

A 32-year-old man is expected to appear in the Durban Magistrates’ Court on Monday 16 November, after police seized about 320kg of cannabis in Westville, Durban. Police estimate the street value of the dagga at R1.5 million.

Police say the arrest followed a multidisciplinary operation involving the Durban Flying Squad, Durban Metro Police and a private security company. Officers had received intelligence that a large consignment of cannabis was being transported along the N2 from north of Durban.

How the arrest happened

Officers tracked the vehicle along the N2 until the driver turned onto the M19. The vehicle then came to a stop, and the driver allegedly abandoned it and ran into nearby bushes. After an extensive search of the area, the suspect was found and arrested.

A search of the vehicle turned up the cannabis, which police put at about 320kg.

Charge and court appearance

The man has been charged with dealing in dagga. He is expected to appear in the Durban Magistrates’ Court on Monday 16 November 2026.

Source: police information, as reported by SurgeZirc SA.
