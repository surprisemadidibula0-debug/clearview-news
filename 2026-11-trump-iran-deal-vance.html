Trump says US does not need an Iran deal, sidelines Vance from talks

President Donald Trump has told Vice President JD Vance to focus on alleged fraud at home instead of negotiations to end the war with Iran.

By Rolivhuwa · 15 November 2026 · 2 min read

US President Donald Trump said on Thursday that the United States does not need a deal with Iran, and told Vice President JD Vance to turn away from negotiations to end the war.

Trump said Vance should instead give “his complete and total attention” to alleged crime and corruption in Democratic-run cities and states. Vance had played a role in efforts to resolve the war. Trump defended the war as necessary to stop Iran from getting a nuclear weapon.

Where the talks stand

On Tuesday, Secretary of State Marco Rubio said after meeting US allies in Paris that Iran’s demands for a deal were “absurd”, pointing to Tehran’s demand for reparations for the war. He said Trump would not sign a bad deal.

Reports said Vance had been considering another round of direct talks with Iranian officials before Trump’s announcement.

Tensions in the Gulf

The announcement comes as tensions around the Strait of Hormuz stay high. Iran said on Saturday that three more tankers hit mines there, a claim the US disputes. Read our report on the tanker incidents.

Sources: Reuters and ABC News reports.
